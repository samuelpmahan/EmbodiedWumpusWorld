02 / Infer

What do I know here?

    Pit probability is not total danger. A zero here does not rule out a Wumpus.

    These beliefs assume independent pits, a safe entrance, and a perfect breeze sensor. Repeated readings of the same breeze are not independent new evidence.

    Start here / No exam

    It comes back one move at a time.

    You can’t see the cave. You get local clues and use them to decide where to go.

    1. Notice an absence. No breeze means the adjacent squares have no pits. Diagonals don’t count.
    2. Make one move. Press Forward. In the practice cave you’ll encounter a breeze. It says “a pit is nearby,” not which square.
    3. Inspect a possibility. Select a neighboring square, then choose “Square evidence” in a side view. See what the observation rules out.
    4. Question your prior. Open “Prior experiment.” Keep the breeze fixed and change what you assumed before sensing it.

    The job

    Grab glittering gold, return to (1,1), then climb. Turning changes your heading; Forward moves one square in that direction.

    The stakes

    Each action costs 1 point. Your first shot costs 10 total and flies straight along your heading. A pit or live Wumpus kills you (−1000). Exiting with gold earns +1000. Climbing at the entrance exits even without gold.
